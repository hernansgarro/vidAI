## Cost model / terminology

Let `C` be live organisms, `P` their total physical parts (up to `MAX_PARTS=7` each), `J` their joints, `E` total eyes, `R=5E` optical rays/tick, `I` optical/environment index entries (food + eggs + living parts), `F` nutrient/carrion count, `G` eggs, `M` mouths, `H` hidden neurons/organism, `N` neural input count/organism, `O` neural outputs/organism, `A` energy-active observer entries, `K` broadphase candidate contacts, `V` visible rendered objects/parts, and `Q` Worker frames/s. Bounds are workload-dependent; buckets and rays may degrade with density, overlapping AABBs, and long ray corridors. World area alone is not a CPU-cost proxy.

At ordinary 60-Hz logical ticks with `NUM_SUBSTEPS=4`, physics advances at 240 substeps/simulated second. Each substep invokes contact resolution with **up to** eight contact passes (early exit on zero contact), and each body velocity solver makes ten joint iterations. Thus up to `60 × 4 × 8 = 1,920` broadphase/contact passes and `60 × 4 × 10 = 2,400` joint-solver sweeps per simulated second are *static loop ceilings*, not CPU frequency measurements or mandatory work counts. The Worker's finite modes are paced; MAX is yielding/unbounded by requested speed, not proven CPU-efficient.

### 2.1 Scheduling-specific constraints

`simulation.worker.ts:57–81` runs tick batches under an ~8 ms deadline or `MAX_BATCH_TICKS=256`, yields through MessageChannel and uses a paced timeout otherwise. Logical ticks and observer frame transport are distinct. `workerPacing.ts:1–13` defines fixed 1/60 s ticks; do not equate requested x20 with achieved x20. `App.tsx:1046–1115` has a capped main-thread fallback stepping loop for MAX; `App.tsx:1715–1727` still draws per RAF. Source reasoning cannot establish actual CPU core usage, FPS or frame jitter. Even off-camera creatures must complete all authoritative physics, perception and ecology.

### Existing protections / false leads

Already present at the pinned SHA: deterministic PRNG and fixed ticks; ordered spatial indexing with unique source-order candidate delivery; reusable SAT polygon buffers and bounded contact-cell pool; typed physics scratch; compiled, invalidatable neural topology with somatic buffers; Worker frame backpressure (one pending); selective neural/lineage frame payloads; reusable offscreen world canvas; visible culling for caustics, food and eggs; self-tube geometry caching. A future optimization proposal must show incremental benefit over these existing mechanisms and must not weaken their invariants.

## 2.10 Trade-off register

- **A — Exact, potentially acceptable:** cache/count/intern precomputed **unchanging** metadata; reusable transient index/arrays if ordering and lifetime are proved; O(A·C) ID lookups using a per-phase set; no-op culling for offscreen drawing (never offscreen simulation); fewer incidental full-frame allocations with byte-for-byte-equivalent replica/epochs; reduced immutable anatomy filtering at draw. All require exact snapshot/PRNG/energy/feeding/optics/learning/transport checks, plus browser output checks where relevant. A is *not* automatically bitwise safe.
- **B — Observer-only, explicit visual decision:** fewer per-part glow strokes, aggregated transient signals, lower observer frame rate, simplified full-canvas decorations or caustic effects; must visibly preserve truthful gains/losses, selection, layering, perceived activity and pause behavior. Controlled equivalent-state comparison first. No signal can be relabeled as a physiological measurement.
- **C — Approximate physical/biological change, separate authorization:** fewer joint/contact iterations, changed dt, rays/precision/tie handling, neural recurrence approximation, reduced body collision/contact geometry, modified fluid equations or edible objects. These can change trajectories, genotypes selected by physical survival, fed joules or historical continuation. **Not authorized by audit.**
- **D — Invalid shortcut:** simulating only the viewport; skipping hungry bodies or eyes; donating energy/proximity feeding; turning off realistic loss accounting; reducing safety checks or restoring frozen results to fabricate speedups; letting the render replica issue authoritative state/exports; optimizing by silently changing population caps, food rates, founders or save semantics.
